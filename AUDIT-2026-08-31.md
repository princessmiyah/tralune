# Issues and observations

## Bugs / likely bugs

1. **Timezone is wrong on the substance log date field.** It prefills "31 Aug 2026 at 5:36 am" while the phone clock reads 3:36 pm — exactly 10 hours out, so it's rendering UTC instead of local time. The symptom log and clinician summary both use correct local time (3:26 pm, 3:38 pm), so it's isolated to that one field. Entries logged now would be stamped with the wrong day for anything after 2 pm.

2. **"Over time" chart shows only two points** for a check-in with five scores. Sleep, Tremor and Anxiety appear to be missing or hidden. Worth checking whether it's plotting all series.

3. **Chart renders as scattered dots, not lines.** Fine with one data point; confirm it connects once there are several.

## Design / UX

4. **The disclaimer is heavy.** Five lines, verbatim, on all four sub-pages plus the hub. On a phone it eats roughly a third of the first screen every time. Suggest full text on the hub, a one-line version with a "why?" tap-through on sub-pages.

5. **Substance "Type" defaults to Stimulants.** A pre-selected class invites mis-tagged entries. Better as a blank "Select…" that forces a choice.

6. **Two different pickers for the same taxonomy.** The scheduler uses a nice sheet with example substances under each class; the substance log uses a bare native dropdown with no examples. Same five classes — make them one component.

7. **Timeline starts at 06:00.** Withdrawal is often worst between about 2 am and 5 am, and that's exactly when someone would open this app for something to do. Consider a full 24 hours, or at least a "show earlier hours" control.

8. **Red flags prompt only after saving.** Confusion and hallucinations in withdrawal can be signs of a medical emergency. Prompting the moment the chip is tapped — before Save — would be safer.

9. **Export is .txt only.** A GP or detox intake will more likely accept a PDF. Also consider a share sheet, not just download.

## Content / clinical framing

10. **Cannabis is grouped under Depressants.** It's usually classified separately. Minor, but a clinician reading the export may notice.

11. **"Create new with AI" is the riskiest thing in this module.** It sits inside an addiction/withdrawal flow, and the whole app is built on a promise not to generate dosing or taper guidance. Needs hard guardrails so a user describing a substance can't get anything back that reads as dosing, timing, or "safe amount" advice.

12. **No visible privacy or storage note.** This is health data plus substance use — arguably the most sensitive combination in the app. Users will want to know where it's stored, whether it's local or synced, and how to delete it. Worth an explicit line before someone logs their first entry.

13. **Summary header says "Patient-generated."** Accurate, and good framing for a clinician. Consider also stamping a date range on the export.
