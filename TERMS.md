# Terms and privacy

*Plain-language draft. This is a starting point written for clarity, not a legal document — have it reviewed by a lawyer before Tralune is released publicly, particularly given it handles health and substance-use information in Australia.*

Last updated: 31 August 2026

## What Tralune is

Tralune is a personal logging and time-structuring tool. It gives you a place to record what you took, how you're feeling, and what you're doing with your day.

## What Tralune is not

**Tralune is a logging and time-structuring tool only. It does not generate, recommend, or calculate taper schedules, doses, or dosing intervals. Withdrawal can be dangerous — please manage it under medical supervision. In an emergency call 000 (Australia).**

Tralune does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is not a substitute for a doctor, a nurse, a pharmacist, a detox service, or a crisis line. Nothing in the app should be treated as clinical guidance, and no activity suggested by the app is prescribed or personalised to your medical situation.

Never delay seeking medical help because of anything in this app.

## Emergency

If you or someone else is in danger, call **000** (Australia).

Other Australian support:

- National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline — 1800 250 015
- Lifeline — 13 11 14
- Beyond Blue — 1300 22 4636

## Your data

Everything you enter into Tralune is stored **locally on your own device**, in your browser's storage.

- It is not uploaded to any server.
- It is not shared with anyone.
- It is not used for advertising, analytics, or training.
- No account is required and none is created.

This means two things worth understanding. Your data is private — nobody else can see it, including us. It is also fragile: if you clear your browser data, use a different device or browser, or use a private window, the data will not be there.

The clinician summary export is generated on your device and saved to your device. It goes nowhere else unless you choose to send it to someone.

## Sharing your summary

If you share a clinician summary with a GP, detox service, or anyone else, that copy is out of the app's hands and is governed by whatever that person or service does with it. Think about who you send it to.

## Accuracy

Everything in Tralune is self-reported by you. The app does not verify it and cannot tell whether it is complete or accurate. Any clinician reading a Tralune summary should treat it as patient-reported information, which is exactly how the export describes itself.

## Using Tralune

You need to be 18 or over. Use it for your own personal record-keeping. Don't rely on it as a medical device — it isn't one, and it is not registered as one.

## Changes

If these terms change materially, the change will be noted here with a new date.
